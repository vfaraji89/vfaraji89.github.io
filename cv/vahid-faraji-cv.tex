\section{Summary}
AI-native architect with 8 years in industry (2 in applied AI, 6 in data products and business analysis) plus 4 years
in research, now designing and shipping production LLM agents end to end. I start from the operational problem: I
work with operators and domain experts, find the highest-leverage workflow, design the agent and its harness
(execution loop, tools, context and memory), and ship it with the evals that prove it works. Fluent in agent patterns,
RAG, prompt engineering, tool use (MCP) and evaluation. I build full-stack in TypeScript, Next.js and the Vercel AI SDK,
and in Python, on the Anthropic Platform (Claude Agent SDK), OpenAI and Google AI, with a production focus on
observability, reliability and token cost. Published at ACM CAIS~'26; ACM Professional Member.
