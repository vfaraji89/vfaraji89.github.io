Enterprise launch August 2026

ProjectLens: pricing, from Excel habit to auditable flow

Shipped with the Pricing & Revenue team at Kariyer.net. ProjectLens takes a project request and carries it end to end: effort calculation, pricing, proposal preparation, approval and follow-up, instead of spawning a fresh Excel file per quote. It plugs into Microsoft Teams and Azure DevOps so requests arrive where the team already works, and ships a read-only SQL console for the questions the dashboards do not answer. 91% of the manual effort removed: 64 hours of hand work down to 5.5, freeing 59 hours (7.3 working days) and avoiding 31 spreadsheets.

Applied AI × Pricing & Revenue · Kariyer.net · internal tool
