How tools are chosen and ranked

A tool is listed when it is used in production or clearly emerging, and when what is said about it can be checked in public sources. Star counts and the Popular, Trending and New badges are calculated every week from GitHub’s public numbers and the tool’s launch date. An Emerging tag always comes with a dated reason and a source.
